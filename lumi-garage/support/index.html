Support

Found a bug, lost progress or have an idea? Write to lumisupport52@gmail.com — please mention your device model and iOS version.

If the “No ads” purchase did not arrive, open Settings → Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Progress

Your progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes the progress.
